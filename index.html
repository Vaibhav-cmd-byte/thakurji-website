Our Story

A Legacy Rolled in Ghee

At Thakur Ji Sweets, every batch begins the same way it always has — pure desi ghee, slow hands and no shortcuts. Our kaarigars roast, fry and shape each sweet the traditional way, so the jalebi still crackles and the laddu still melts exactly as it should.

The name Thakur Ji carries a promise: sweets made with the same care you'd offer at home, worthy of a festival table or a Prasad thali alike. From our counter on Birhana Road, that promise travels across Kanpur, one box at a time.

"Meetha jo banaye, har pal yaadgaar."
