Privacy Policy
Shop Game for Android

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own phone.

Who we are

This policy covers the Thakur Ji Sweets shop game for Android (package com.thakurjisweets.mithaiempire), published by Vaibhav Singh for Thakur Ji Sweets, Kanodia Building, 58/74, Birhana Road, Naughara, Kanpur 208001, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Information we collect

None. The game has no account, login, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal data.

Data saved on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example coins, shop levels, story choices, sound and vibration settings) are saved in a file inside the game's private storage on your phone. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration – used only for touch feedback in the game. It cannot read any data.

The game does not use the internet while you play.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising or analytics code. Google Play, which delivers the game to you, works under Google's own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the date above will change before that version is released.
